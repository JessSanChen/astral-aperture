## Search scope and limits

Search performed on 21 October 2026. Query families: active search versus surveying/testing; multifidelity error discovery; active feature acquisition and value of information; active/prediction-powered inference and robust sampling; selective labels and off-policy confidence sequences; contextual LLM cascades; AI-control games and adaptive monitor testing; subgroup/unknown-unknown discovery; executable agent benchmarks. Backward references and searches for newer related papers were included.

Primary papers, proceedings, and author repositories were used for substantive claims. This is a critical research review, not an exhaustive systematic review, a replication, or a proof audit. Publication status is stated only where independently verified from a primary record. A paper license does not imply that code, weights, or datasets share that license. Availability does not imply that an artifact was executed successfully.

## Compact branch-status export

- **Branch, date, artifact version:** ASTRAL B; 21 October 2026; v0.1.
- **Current question and status:** Proposed lead: can action-specific inspection value generalize across unseen workflow families for fixed, fallible judges? Investigating B is user-approved; this precise question and method are not approved or tested.
- **Threat model and observation boundary:** Retrospective monitoring of safe fictional workflows. A bounded actor controls permitted messages/actions in the sandbox, not the oracle or audit randomness. Defender retrieves authorized records, calls fixed judges, stops, flags, or audits; it does not block or reverse actions. No hidden scratchpad, unauthorized history, or evaluator-only state is available.
- **Nearest prior work, exact overlap, source links:** ACO (conditional-loss acquisition), Learning-To-Measure (cross-task acquisition), TREACLE (contextual model/prompt routing), MICRO (multifidelity confirmed-error discovery); source URLs above. Active Testing/ASI/Robust Sampling/OPAL/Ao et al. already cover major selective-audit inference ideas.
- **Proposed scientific difference and evidence for the gap:** Test whether counterfactual correction/corruption and evidence-accessibility structure yields transferable acquisition policies for fixed transcript monitors, under independently executable labels. This is a plausible empirical gap, not established algorithmic novelty.
- **Main baseline and decisive experiment:** Strong action-aware loss-regression/ACO-style lookahead, risk ranking plus audits, contextual cascade, and high-inspection references. Held-out-family test with equal information access and complete cost accounting; oracle-selector headroom measured first.
- **Dataset/oracle provenance and leakage controls:** Independently implemented fictional workflow state machines and explicit rule predicates; separate text-rendering audit; selected public safe tasks with revalidated predicates; split by base world, task family, renderer, model lineage, and development history. Full truth is evaluator-only.
- **Results with uncertainty:** Not yet run. Literature results are author-reported, not independently replicated here.
- **Supported claim and claim not supported:** Supported now: generic routing plus random audits is insufficient novelty. Not supported: effectiveness of the proposed controller, deployment error rates, biological-misuse detection, harm prevention, or publication acceptance.
- **Required shared interface/artifact:** Versioned trace/policy/judge/action/oracle/evaluation records; distinct inspection and audit probabilities; immutable pre-audit decisions; local replay and reporting.
- **Independent dependencies and unresolved access issues:** ASTRAL code/configuration, pinned model access/licenses, independent environment/oracle engineering, adjudicator recruitment, statistical review, storage and inference orchestration. C and partners are not dependencies.
- **Stop/pivot criterion and current recommendation:** Stop new-method claims if strong simple/action-aware baselines match across held-out conditions, available evidence yields negligible repair headroom, independent labels fail, or the mechanism disappears outside a shared renderer. Recommend a decisive B1 pilot; use estimation as infrastructure and family discovery as a separately scored secondary question.
- **User-confirmed decisions to add to brief:** No new decisions. The existing parallel-pursuit, partner-independence, resource, and checkpoint decisions remain in force. This proposal does not update the human-reviewed decision log.
- **Next experiment:** Build the paired evidence-accessibility pilot, enumerate a bounded inspection tree for fixed judges, and test held-out action-value predictability against strong simple baselines before selecting a complex controller.
